# Research input

The project started from a secondary synthesis: *Evidence-based typography for sustained digital reading*. Its research cutoff is 11 November 2026, and its full report contains 44 references.

- Only the **summary** of that synthesis was available while building v0.1. The full `.docx` report, with all 44 references, was not.
- The summary's claims and sources were extracted into `evidence/registry.json` as source `S-synthesis-2026`, with status `unverified-secondary`.
- Every primary source we could identify was checked against the original, as far as access allowed. See [`evidence/VERIFICATION.md`](../evidence/VERIFICATION.md) for the checks and for the **discrepancies found between the synthesis and the originals**.
- The synthesis text itself is not redistributed here. Its rights belong to its author.
- To add the missing references, use the "New study extraction" issue form. List the reference among the registry gaps until it is verified.
